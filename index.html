Maxfiylik siyosati

Ushbu siyosat "Hoja Nasriddin" mobil o'yini (iOS va Android, keyingi o'rinlarda — "O'yin") qanday ma'lumotlardan foydalanishini tushuntiradi. O'yin ishlab chiquvchisi — ios89dev.

1. Biz o'zimiz yig'adigan ma'lumotlar

O'yin hisob (akkaunt) ochishni talab qilmaydi va sizning ismingiz, telefon raqamingiz, email manzilingiz yoki joylashuvingizni so'ramaydi. O'yin jarayoni — o'tilgan levellar, yulduzlar, tangalar, kiyim va sozlamalar — faqat qurilmangizning o'zida saqlanadi va bizga yuborilmaydi.

2. Reklama (Google AdMob)

O'yin bepul va Google AdMob orqali reklama ko'rsatadi. Reklama ko'rsatish uchun Google quyidagi ma'lumotlarni yig'ishi va ishlatishi mumkin:

  • reklama identifikatori (iOS'da IDFA — faqat ruxsat bersangiz; Android'da reklama ID'si);
  • IP manzil va undan taxminiy joylashuv (mamlakat/shahar darajasida);
  • qurilma va tizim ma'lumotlari (model, operatsion tizim, til);
  • reklama bilan bog'liq harakatlar (ko'rish, bosish) va nosozlik ma'lumotlari.

Bu ma'lumotlar reklamani ko'rsatish, o'lchash, firibgarlikka qarshi kurash va (rozilik bo'lsa) reklamani shaxsiylashtirish uchun ishlatiladi. Google ma'lumotlardan qanday foydalanishi haqida: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites va Google maxfiylik siyosati.

3. Rozilik va tanlov

  • Yevropa (EEA), Buyuk Britaniya va Shveytsariya: O'yin ochilganda Google'ning rozilik oynasi chiqadi. Rozilik bermasangiz, faqat shaxsiylashtirilmagan reklama ko'rsatiladi. Qaroringizni istalgan vaqtda bosh menyudagi "Reklama sozlamalari" tugmasi orqali o'zgartirishingiz mumkin.
  • iOS: O'yin reklama identifikatoridan (IDFA) foydalanishdan oldin Apple'ning "kuzatuvga ruxsat" (App Tracking Transparency) so'rovini ko'rsatadi. Rad etsangiz ham O'yin to'liq ishlaydi. Ruxsatni keyin Sozlamalar → Maxfiylik va xavfsizlik → Kuzatish bo'limida o'zgartirish mumkin.
  • Android: Reklama ID'sini qurilma sozlamalarida (Google → Reklamalar) o'chirish yoki yangilash mumkin.

4. Mukofotli reklamalar

Ba'zi reklamalarni o'zingiz tanlab ko'rasiz (masalan, qo'shimcha jon yoki tanga ×2 evaziga). Ular ixtiyoriy; ko'rmasangiz ham O'yinni oxirigacha o'ynash mumkin.

5. Bolalar

6. Ma'lumotlarni saqlash va o'chirish

Qurilmadagi o'yin ma'lumotlari O'yin o'chirilganda o'chib ketadi. Google yig'gan reklama ma'lumotlari Google siyosatiga muvofiq saqlanadi; ularni boshqarish uchun yuqoridagi sozlamalardan foydalaning yoki myadcenter.google.com sahifasiga kiring.

Privacy Policy

This policy explains how the mobile game "Hoja Nasriddin" (iOS and Android, the "Game") uses information. The Game is developed by ios89dev.

1. Information we collect ourselves

The Game does not require an account and does not ask for your name, phone number, email address or location. Game progress — completed levels, stars, coins, outfits and settings — is stored only on your device and is not sent to us.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Game is free and shows ads through Google AdMob. To serve ads, Google may collect and use:

  • an advertising identifier (on iOS the IDFA — only if you allow it; on Android the Advertising ID);
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it (country/city level);
  • device and system information (model, operating system, language);
  • ad interactions (views, taps) and diagnostic data.

This information is used to deliver and measure ads, prevent fraud and, with consent, personalize ads. Learn how Google uses data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and the Google Privacy Policy.

3. Consent and your choices

  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: Google's consent form is shown when the Game starts. If you do not consent, only non-personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time with the "Reklama sozlamalari" (Ad settings) button in the main menu.
  • iOS: Before using the advertising identifier (IDFA), the Game shows Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. The Game works fully if you decline. You can change this later in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Android: You can delete or reset your Advertising ID in device settings (Google → Ads).

4. Rewarded ads

Some ads are watched only if you choose to (for example, for an extra life or double coins). They are optional; the Game can be completed without them.

5. Children

The Game is intended for users aged 13 and over and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

6. Retention and deletion

Game data on your device is removed when you uninstall the Game. Advertising data collected by Google is retained under Google's policies; use the settings above or visit myadcenter.google.com to manage it.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, this page will be updated and the date above will change.

8. Contact

For questions or data requests: unistatdeveloper@gmail.com
